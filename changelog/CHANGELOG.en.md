# Changelog

## 1.0 – November 12, 2026
- First version: build towers in 3D, the crane swings, a tap drops the block
- Perfect drops give bonuses and combos and keep the tower steady; crooked blocks make it sway
- Five modes: Quick Game, Time Attack, Challenge with 15 levels, Build City and Party for two players
- Parachuting residents move in, stars and dust clouds when a block lands
- Original sound effects and music, vibration on landing
- Day and night look with lit windows in the skyline
- Hall of Fame with your best towers, record lines during play
- "What's New" and update check like in OpeniWeather
